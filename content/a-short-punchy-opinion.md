A short take should fit in the time it takes the kettle to boil. This one is a sample of that shape, not an opinion about a real event.

The words are a prop. They are not a slogan, and they are not attributed to anyone.

Three things this file is for:

1. Showing the short-take section has its own page.
2. Showing up in the stream with the other posts.
3. Proving a wikilink back to [[A Placeholder Headline That Argues Something Boldly|the sample essay]] resolves.

That is the whole take. When a real one replaces it, keep the same frontmatter and write the thing you actually think.
