This file is a sample of a guest piece. Sample Guest is a label, the way “Example Source” would be a label. There is no person by that name to quote, credit, or look up.

The byline should read Sample Guest, and a short box under it should say Guest contributor. Chuck’s own pieces do not get that box. Their byline stays Chuck Houseworth, which is the default when `author` is left off.

A guest can still link to something Chuck wrote. The [[A Placeholder Headline That Argues Something Boldly|sample essay]] is one of those. The link works because that essay is published. A draft would have stayed plain text.
